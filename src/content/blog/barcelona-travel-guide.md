Barcelona works best when you combine architecture with neighborhood time. Plan Gaudi sights early, then use the rest of the day to walk, eat and explore nearby streets rather than constantly crossing the city.

## Plan by area

Group the Gothic Quarter and nearby central sights, then give another block to Eixample and its architecture. Keep beach time separate so the itinerary remains flexible.

## Gaudi planning

Popular attractions can require timed entry. Check the official venue websites for current opening information and book only the sites that truly need reservations.

## Food and neighborhoods

Barcelona's food scene ranges from markets and bakeries to restaurants worth reserving. Leave at least one meal open so you can follow local recommendations.

## Practical tip

Pick accommodation near a metro connection. The exact neighborhood matters less than having easy access to the areas you plan to visit.
