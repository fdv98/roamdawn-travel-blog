Dubrovnik's historic setting is spectacular, but its popularity changes the best way to plan a visit. Early starts, realistic walking plans and time outside the busiest streets can make a major difference.

## Old Town first

Use your first morning for the historic core and main viewpoints. Check current entry and operating information for attractions before committing to a timed plan.

## Walk beyond the headline sights

The city walls and central streets are only part of the experience. Leave a quieter block for waterfront walks, local restaurants or a nearby viewpoint.

## Day trips

Boat excursions and nearby coastal stops can add variety, but verify current schedules directly with operators because routes may be seasonal.

## Best planning habit

Avoid changing accommodation repeatedly along the coast. Dubrovnik works better as a focused base when your trip is short.
