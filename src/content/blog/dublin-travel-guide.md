Dublin is compact enough for a walking-focused trip, but the best experience comes from balancing headline sights with neighborhoods, food and time by the river.

## Build by area

Keep central historic sights together, then use another block for museums and parks. Check venue schedules before visiting and leave a flexible afternoon.

## Food and neighborhoods

Markets, bakeries and pubs can be as useful for understanding Dublin as museums. Choose a neighborhood and explore it without a checklist.

## How long to stay

Two to three full days make a useful city break. With more time, use Dublin as a base for a carefully chosen excursion rather than trying to add several far-away stops.

## Practical tip

Check current public transport information and opening hours shortly before your trip. Travel times and schedules can change.
