Skye is a landscape-driven destination. The goal is not to see every famous viewpoint but to build a route that leaves time for weather, walking and the roads between places.

## Start with a regional map

Group stops by area and keep one flexible block for the weather. Popular places can be busy, especially during peak periods.

## Driving matters

Road conditions and narrow sections can slow progress. Avoid planning a late arrival after a long sightseeing day.

## Hiking and safety

Check current forecasts and local notices before exposed walks. Carry suitable footwear and follow marked routes and local guidance.

## How long?

Two or three nights gives most first-time visitors a better experience than a single rushed night.
