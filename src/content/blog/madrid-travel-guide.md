Madrid rewards a balanced itinerary. Its museums, parks, food markets and neighborhoods are spread across a manageable central area, so the goal is to explore deeply rather than rush between landmarks.

## Museum planning

Choose one major museum block per day and pair it with nearby streets or a park. Verify current opening hours and reservation requirements directly with each institution.

## Neighborhood time

Malasana, La Latina, Salamanca and other areas each give the city a different character. A neighborhood walk can be the best part of a day after a museum-heavy morning.

## Food

Markets are useful for sampling different foods, while traditional restaurants reward a little research. Reserve only where needed and leave room for an unplanned meal.

## Day trips

Madrid can support day trips, but do not add one automatically. A slower city day can be more enjoyable if your schedule is already full.
