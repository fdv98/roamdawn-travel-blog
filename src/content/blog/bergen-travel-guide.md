Bergen is both a destination and a gateway. Its historic harbor and surrounding hills make it worthwhile to explore before moving into the fjord region.

## Bryggen and the center

Start around the harbor and historic core, then use a second block for museums, neighborhoods and food. Check current attraction hours before visiting.

## Viewpoints

The surrounding hills offer classic city views, but weather can change quickly. Keep your day flexible and confirm current conditions if you plan a longer walk.

## Connections to the fjords

Bergen works well as a starting point for rail, ferry or road journeys. Compare current travel times rather than choosing a connection only because it looks short on a map.

## How long?

Two to three nights is a useful starting point, with longer stays if Bergen is also your base for nearby excursions.
