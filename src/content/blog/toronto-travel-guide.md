Toronto is best understood through neighborhoods. A first trip can combine downtown landmarks with distinct local areas, food and waterfront time.

## Build by neighborhood

Keep major central sights together, then use another day for neighborhoods farther from the core. This makes transit time more manageable.

## Food planning

The city's diversity makes food a central part of the trip. Explore one neighborhood market or food district rather than treating meals as quick stops between attractions.

## Waterfront and museums

Balance indoor and outdoor activities. Weather can shift, and museums provide useful alternatives when a planned waterfront afternoon is not comfortable.

## Getting around

Use current transit information when planning. The fastest route for one neighborhood may not be the best option at another time of day.
