Oslo works well as a calm city break or as the first stop before heading toward Norway's fjords. Its museums, waterfront and green spaces make it easy to mix indoor and outdoor days.

## Start by the waterfront

Aker Brygge and nearby areas provide an easy first orientation. Add one museum or cultural stop instead of stacking several in one afternoon.

## Museums and neighborhoods

Choose museums by interest and geography. The city's compact transport network makes it possible to explore different districts without turning every day into a transit project.

## Season matters

Winter daylight and outdoor conditions differ greatly from summer. Check current attraction hours, weather and transport before setting a detailed schedule.

## How long?

Two to three full days works well for a first look before continuing into Norway.
