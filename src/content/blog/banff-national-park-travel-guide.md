Banff National Park is a place where the landscape sets the schedule. Weather, road conditions, trail access and seasonal demand can all affect a day.

## Choose a base

Banff town works for many first trips, while Lake Louise can make sense when specific scenery is the priority. Check current accommodation and park information before booking.

## Plan around daylight and conditions

Outdoor days should have a backup. Weather can shift quickly, especially around mountain areas, and some routes may have seasonal restrictions.

## Respect park rules

Stay on marked trails, follow wildlife guidance and use official Parks Canada information for current alerts and reservations.

## Do not overbook

A few major lake or trail experiences with flexible time are better than trying to cover every viewpoint in one day.
