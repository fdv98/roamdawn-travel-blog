Seville is best enjoyed slowly. The historic center is rich enough for several days, while warm weather can make a packed walking itinerary unnecessarily tiring.

## Start with the historic core

Group the cathedral area, historic lanes and main plazas into one broad sightseeing block. Check official attraction information for opening hours and timed-entry requirements.

## Plan for the heat

In warmer periods, place longer walks in the morning or late afternoon and use shaded or indoor stops around the middle of the day.

## Explore beyond landmarks

Triana and neighborhood markets can provide a different view of the city. Leave time for a long lunch or evening walk rather than adding another distant attraction.

## Route idea

Three days can work well: one day for the monumental center, one for neighborhoods and food, and one flexible day for a museum, garden or nearby excursion.
