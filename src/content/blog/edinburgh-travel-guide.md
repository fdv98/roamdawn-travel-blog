Edinburgh packs a lot into a walkable center. The best itinerary balances historic streets and viewpoints with museums, gardens and quieter neighborhood time.

## Old Town

Start with the Royal Mile area, then branch into closes, viewpoints and nearby attractions. Popular periods can be busy, so earlier starts are useful.

## New Town

New Town adds a different architectural character and works well as a second-day walking area. Combine it with gardens and museums rather than repeatedly crossing the center.

## Weather planning

Rain and wind can change the feel of a viewpoint-heavy day. Keep an indoor museum or café route as a backup.

## How long to stay

Three full days are enough for a strong first visit. With more time, Edinburgh can become the gateway to a carefully planned Scotland road trip.
