Vancouver is unusual in the best way: city neighborhoods, ocean and mountain landscapes can all fit into one trip. Build days around geography rather than crossing the region repeatedly.

## Neighborhood planning

Use one day for downtown and the waterfront, another for Stanley Park and nearby neighborhoods, and a separate block for mountain or coastal excursions.

## Nature access

Outdoor conditions can change. Check current park, trail and weather information before committing to hikes or mountain roads.

## Food and city time

Vancouver's neighborhoods are diverse, so do not plan every meal around downtown. Choose an area and explore it properly.

## How long?

Three to five days works well for a first trip, with longer stays allowing more flexible outdoor days.
