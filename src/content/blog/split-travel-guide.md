Split is both an attractive city and a practical base for the Dalmatian coast. Give the historic core time, then use day trips carefully instead of treating the city as a place to sleep.

## Diocletian's Palace

The old center is compact, but it is more enjoyable when explored without a strict checklist. Walk the lanes, waterfront and market areas, and check official opening information for museums and attractions.

## Beaches and waterfront time

Keep one slower block for the coast. A relaxed afternoon can balance a history-heavy morning.

## Day trips

Nearby islands and coastal towns can be reached by ferry or boat, but schedules vary by season. Confirm the current operator timetable before relying on a tight connection.

## How long to stay

Two or three nights can work for a first visit, especially if Split is one stop in a longer Croatia route.
